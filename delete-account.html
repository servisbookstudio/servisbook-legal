How to permanently remove your account and data from ServisBook

How to Delete Your Account

You can delete your ServisBook account and all associated data directly within the app. Follow these steps:

1
Open ServisBook on your device and log in.
2
Tap the Settings icon in the bottom navigation bar.
3
Scroll down to the Account section and tap Delete Account.
4
Confirm the deletion when prompted. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

Alternatively, you can remove all data by uninstalling the app from your device. Because all business data is stored only on your device, uninstalling removes it completely and permanently.

What Gets Deleted vs. Retained

Data typeWhere storedOn account deletion
Clients, service records, appointments Your device only Deleted immediately
Photos of equipment Your device only Deleted immediately
Company information & settings Your device only Deleted immediately
Reminders & notification settings Your device only Deleted immediately
Subscription record & anonymous device ID Firebase Firestore (Google LLC) Retained while subscription is active, then deleted within 90 days after the subscription expires or account deletion is requested, whichever is later
Anonymous crash reports & diagnostics Firebase Crashlytics (Google LLC) Retained up to 90 days per Firebase policy, then deleted
Anonymous analytics events Firebase Analytics (Google LLC) Retained up to 14 months per Firebase policy (no personal identifiers)
