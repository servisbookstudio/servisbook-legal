ServisBook

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026
Short version: ServisBook stores all your workshop data (clients, service records, photos) exclusively on your device. We never upload your business data to any server. We collect only minimal analytics and crash reports to improve the app.

1. Who We Are

ServisBook ("the app", "we", "us") is a workshop management application developed by Branislav Krstić. If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us at servisbook.studio@gmail.com.

2. Data Stored Only on Your Device

The following data is stored exclusively in a local database on your device. It is never transmitted to our servers or any third party:

  • Client information (name, phone number, email, address, notes)
  • Service records (descriptions, dates, prices, status)
  • Photos of equipment taken within the app
  • Company information (name, address, working hours, workstations)
  • Reminder schedules and notification settings

You have full control over this data. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

4. Permissions

ServisBook requests the following device permissions. All sensitive permissions are requested only when you explicitly tap the relevant button — never automatically on app launch.

READ_CONTACTS
Used only when you tap "Import from contacts" inside the Add Client screen. The selected contact's name and phone number are pre-filled into the form. No contacts are stored on our servers.
READ_CALL_LOG & READ_PHONE_STATE
Used only when you tap "Import from call log" inside the Add Client screen. The app reads your last 20 incoming calls and displays them in a selection list so you can quickly add a caller as a new client. No call log data is stored beyond the form field, and none is transmitted anywhere.
CAMERA
Used to photograph equipment before and after service. Photos are saved locally on your device only.
READ_MEDIA_IMAGES
Used to select existing photos from your gallery for service records. No images are uploaded to any server.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS · SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM · USE_EXACT_ALARM
Used to deliver service reminders at the exact time you schedule them. All reminder data stays on your device.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED
Used to re-schedule your reminders after the device restarts, so you don't miss notifications.

5. In-App Purchases

ServisBook offers an optional Premium subscription processed entirely by Google Play. We do not collect or store your payment details. When a purchase is completed, Google Play provides us with a purchase token, which we store alongside an anonymous device ID in Firebase Firestore solely to verify your subscription status and prevent fraud.

6. Advertising

The free version of ServisBook displays banner advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google may use data about your device and app interactions to show relevant ads. You can learn more about Google's advertising practices at policies.google.com/privacy. Premium subscribers do not see advertisements.

7. Data Retention

Data stored on your device remains there until you delete it or uninstall the app. Anonymous analytics data collected by Firebase is retained according to Google's standard retention policies (up to 14 months for Analytics). Subscription records in Firebase Firestore are retained while your subscription is active and for a reasonable period afterward for fraud-prevention purposes.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: You can view all data the app stores by browsing within the app itself.
  • Deletion: You can delete all local data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. To request deletion of any data held by our services (such as subscription records), contact us at the address below.
  • Portability: The app includes a backup & restore feature so you can export your data at any time.
  • Opt-out of analytics: You can disable Firebase Analytics data collection by adjusting your device's advertising settings.

For requests related to your rights under GDPR or other applicable privacy laws, please contact us and we will respond within 30 days.

9. Children's Privacy

ServisBook is intended for use by adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can delete it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
